Hello, I am Satoshi Nakamura.
This is my solo final project for DADS five two five zero.
I built an AI assistant for data engineering requests.
Business users often prefer a spreadsheet.
They ask for a report, a data extract, or a BI change.
A business analyst checks the need, value, and timing.
BA means business analyst.
Requests can lose context when people copy between tools.
My goal is to keep the agreed business need clear.
The ticket, changes, tests, and decision stay linked.
This supports a single source of truth for each request.
My app includes two human decisions.
The demo ends with a reviewed patch package.
It does not merge code or release a production change.
